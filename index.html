The doctrine

How these theses think

Bet on the winners, underwrite the threats, own the funding the threats summon.

Only one-of-one strategic monopolies earn a position. Each thesis states its implied yield, names the milestones that move the odds, and carries a downside floor that gets re-run at every earnings print. Noise is a property of the route, never the destination.
